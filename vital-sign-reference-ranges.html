Temperature by measurement method

Reference: Canadian Paediatric Society, 2015. Ranges do not vary meaningfully with age beyond infancy.

MethodNormal range (°C)Normal range (°F)
Rectal36.6–38.097.9–100.4
Tympanic (ear)35.8–38.096.4–100.4
Oral35.5–37.595.9–99.5
Axillary36.5–37.597.7–99.5
Screening methods (axillary, temporal, tympanic) trade accuracy for ease of use. Rectal and oral routes track core temperature most closely. A rectal reading ≥ 38.0 °C (100.4 °F) is the standard definition of fever.

Oxygen saturation (SpO₂)

PopulationNormal range
Adult / child (beyond immediate newborn period)95–100 %
Newborn, first minutes of lifeLower values expected; rises over ~10 minutes
SpO₂ below 90–92 % beyond the immediate newborn period may suggest a respiratory condition or cyanotic congenital heart disease and warrants evaluation.
